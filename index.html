Photosensitive Seizure Warning

A very small percentage of people may experience a seizure or blackout when exposed to certain visual stimuli, including flashing lights, rapidly shifting high-contrast colors, or moving geometric patterns.

This simulation displays continuously moving, rotating colored squares over morphing rainbow backgrounds. Even individuals with no prior history of seizures or epilepsy may have an undiagnosed condition that can trigger photosensitive epileptic seizures.

If you or anyone in your family has an epileptic condition, consult a physician before viewing. Stop viewing immediately and seek medical advice if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitches, loss of awareness, disorientation, or involuntary movements.
